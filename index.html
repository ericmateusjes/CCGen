        About CCGen

        CCGen is a tool for generating concrete constitutive data for the Abaqus Concrete Damaged Plasticity (CDP) model. The software generates the compression hardening, compression damage, tension stiffening and tension damage tables.

        All calculations are performed locally in the browser. No data is sent to a server.

        Conventions

        • Unit system: N, mm and MPa. Compressive stresses and strains are taken as positive.
        • Compression: the tables are expressed in terms of stress and crushing strain εcch (inelastic strain), the latter calculated from the total strain and the stress.
        • Tension: the tables are expressed in terms of stress and cracking strain. When the constitutive law is defined as a function of crack opening, it is converted to strain using the adopted characteristic length.
        • Modulus of elasticity: E0 is the initial elastic modulus used in the strain conversions, according to the selected formulation.
        • Damage: the values are limited to dmax (0.99 by default), which avoids unit values.

        Checks

        The generated data are submitted to automatic consistency checks, including stress and strain limits, monotonicity of the strains, admissible damage values, non-negative and non-decreasing plastic strain and energy consistency, when applicable. The inconsistencies found are shown in the Checks tab and may prevent the export of the data.

        Limitations and caveats

        • Some damage formulations are compatible only with specific constitutive laws. Incompatible combinations are identified by alerts or by export blocking.
        • The mechanical properties estimated from codes and empirical relations are approximations and must be used considering their respective hypotheses and limitations.
        • The fracture energies and characteristic lengths used in the regularization may influence the post-peak response. The mesh sensitivity must be evaluated by the user.
        • CCGen generates the uniaxial material data but does not determine all the parameters required by the CDP model. The remaining parameters must be defined by the user.
        • The automatic checks evaluate the consistency of the generated data, but do not guarantee the physical representativeness of the curves or the convergence of numerical analyses.

        CCGen is a tool to support numerical modelling. It does not replace the experimental characterization of the materials, the validation of the constitutive laws or the verification of the results obtained in Abaqus. The full equations and procedures are in the Documentation tab.
